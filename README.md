## About Us

**Zeltrion Solutions** is an AI-powered marketing, automation, technology and growth agency. We build the systems that help businesses attract, convert and follow up with customers, combining sound marketing strategy with intelligent automation and modern web technology.

Our approach is built on three ideas:

- **Strategy first.** Every solution starts with the business problem and the outcome the client needs.
- **Intelligence built in.** AI agents, automation and smart workflows do the repetitive work so teams can focus on growth.
- **Measured by results.** We track leads, conversions and revenue impact, not vanity metrics.

Founded and led by **Nimra Akbar**, Founder & CEO.
